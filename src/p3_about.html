About Burger Brewery

A burger and craft beer restaurant, built for the pairing.

Burger Brewery is a burger and craft beer restaurant in Bengaluru, India. Every burger is built to be paired with a craft beer.

What it is
A burger and craft beer restaurant. Burgers are treated as a craft, made with honest ingredients to the same quality every time.
Founded
Burger Brewery was founded in 2025.
First outlet
Koramangala 1st Block, Bengaluru. It opened on 4 July 2026.
Next outlet
Gurgaon, opening soon.
Delivery
Through our cloud kitchens across Delhi NCR, Mumbai and Bangalore.
What makes it different
Every burger is paired with a craft beer, so the food and the beer are designed together.
Goal
To build India’s go-to burger and beer brand, with a Burger Brewery in every pin code that deserves a good burger and craft beer on tap.

The investor who got hungry

Maadhav Veer Singh studied finance and economics at Tulane University in the United States and started his career as an investment banker in New York.

After returning to India he joined Stride Asset Management, where he worked on an equity fund focused on manufacturing and emerging technologies. His job was to study businesses and ask what was missing.

In food and beverage, he saw that burgers and beer were among the fastest-growing categories in tier 1 and tier 2 India. Yet there was no burger brand that treated the burger as a craft, with honest ingredients and consistent quality, in a room where you want to sit with friends and a cold beer.

“I could not unsee that gap.”Maadhav Veer Singh, Founder & CEO

That gap became Burger Brewery. The first outlet opened in Koramangala, Bengaluru, on 4 July 2026. The next is coming to Gurgaon, and until then Burger Brewery delivers through its cloud kitchens.
