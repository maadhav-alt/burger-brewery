The bite, then the sip.

You know the bite. Char, melted cheese, a little heat running toward your wrist. Then the sip: cold, sharp, and suddenly you want the next bite. That’s the whole idea. A kitchen built around the bite, a bar built around the sip, and a table nobody’s in a hurry to leave.
